## 📋 Project Overview
The **Arduino Voice Command Blind Stick** is an advanced assistive IoT and smart navigation system designed to empower visually impaired individuals to navigate safely and independently[cite: 7]. Replacing traditional walking canes, this system combines smart sensor feedback with a pair of voice-controlled assistant sunglasses and Google Assistant integration[cite: 7].

---

## 🕶️ 1. Smart Assistant Sunglasses & Mobile Connectivity
* **Voice-Controlled Interface:** Seamlessly control smartphone functions and interact with **Google Assistant** via Bluetooth-connected smart sunglasses[cite: 7].
* **Call Management:** Easily answer and disconnect incoming phone calls on the go[cite: 7].
* **Audio & Entertainment:** Built-in earphones allow users to enjoy music and audio safely even in noisy outdoor environments[cite: 7].
* **GPS Navigation:** Get precise turn-by-turn directions, travel routes, and duration estimates via voice commands using Google Maps[cite: 7].

---

## ⚙️ 2. Smart Stick Core Features & Sensors
* **Obstacle Detection:** Front-mounted ultrasonic sensors detect obstacles meters ahead and warn the user via **Sinhala voice commands**[cite: 7].
* **Terrain & Staircase Safety:** Proximity sensors identify upcoming staircases, slopes, or drops, issuing immediate Sinhala audio warnings[cite: 7].
* **Conductive Liquid Detection:** A soil/moisture sensor at the base detects water or conductive liquid hazards ahead and alerts the user[cite: 7].
* **Metal & Object Detection:** An integrated metal detector identifies iron/metal objects in the path to prevent potential injuries[cite: 7].
* **Emergency Night Light:** Equipped with an emergency LED light so approaching pedestrians can easily spot the visually impaired user at night[cite: 7].

---

## ⚡ 3. Power System, Battery & Cost Specification
* **Energy Harvesting (Piezo & Solar):** Features a **piezoelectric sensor** that generates electricity every time the stick taps the ground, alongside a **solar panel** for eco-friendly charging[cite: 7].
* **Battery & Runtime:** Powered by an **8000mAh battery**, providing **6–7 hours of continuous operation** on a single charge[cite: 7].
* **Power Bank Functionality:** Includes a built-in power bank circuit capable of charging mobile phones in emergency situations[cite: 7].
* **Affordability:** Cost-effective design with an estimated production cost of approximately **Rs. 8,000.00**[cite: 7].
